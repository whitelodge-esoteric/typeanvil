1 Executive summary

The company closed the fourth quarter ahead of plan on both revenue and operating margin. Full-year revenue grew 23.9% to 17,455 kUSD, and operating income more than doubled as the cost structure absorbed the growth without a proportional increase in headcount.

Three themes dominated the quarter: continued expansion within the Business and Enterprise tiers; the successful launch of the reporting workspace, which lifted engagement across every plan; and a disciplined approach to research spending that kept the gross margin trend positive while the roadmap stayed on schedule.

Summary for the board. The quarter confirmed the plan's central assumption — that the wedge features drive retention — and the operating review in section 3 sets out where the next increment of investment should go.

Management recommends approving the FY2027 operating plan, which holds the current cost base and redirects a portion of the savings from the restructuring completed in FY2025 into the enterprise go-to-market program.

3 Operating review

Operating performance improved across all three product lines. The platform line grew fastest, benefiting from the reporting workspace and from the expansion of the enterprise agreements signed in the first half of the year.

The reporting workspace shipped in the second week of the quarter and was adopted by 61% of Business-tier accounts within thirty days. The sales team closed four enterprise agreements, each with a multi-year term, and the pipeline entering the new year is the strongest on record.

The operating review continues in the section on risk and outlook, which sets out the assumptions behind the FY2027 plan and the principal risks to it. Management's view is that the largest single risk remains the concentration of revenue in the platform line, which now represents 63% of total revenue, up from 58% a year earlier.

4 Risk and outlook

The outlook for FY2027 assumes revenue growth of 18% to 22% and a modest expansion of operating margin. The plan holds headcount flat in the first half and adds capacity only where the pipeline justifies it.
