Benchmark for CORE-141 (margin-box styling layer, per-box declarations). The top-center margin box above declares its own font (monospace 12pt), a color, a bottom border, and vertical alignment. Before CORE-141 lands, the engine renders margin boxes at a fixed 10pt Arial with name-derived alignment only — so this box loses its font, color, and border.

Ground truth: the header text renders in a monospace face, dark blue, with a rule under it.
