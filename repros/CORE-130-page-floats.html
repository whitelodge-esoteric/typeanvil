PAGE-TOP FLOAT: this box declares float: top and must pin to the TOP of its page's content box, with body text flowing below it (css-page-3 page floats; CORE-130).

Body text after the page float. Before CORE-130 lands, only inline-direction floats are supported — float: top falls back to an inline float (left side), so this box hugs the left edge instead of pinning to the page top.

Ground truth: the bordered box sits at the top of the content area, full width, with both paragraphs below it.
