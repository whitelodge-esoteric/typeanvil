Operating Manual — Northwind 2400 Pallet Truck

Document NW-2400-OM Rev. C · Applies to serial numbers 2400-10000 and above · Read before use
Safety noticeNever place any part of the body under a raised load. Lower the forks fully before leaving the truck unattended.

This manual describes the safe operation and routine maintenance of the Northwind 2400 hand pallet truck. It is written for operators who have completed the site induction and for maintenance staff performing the checks in section 4. Keep the manual with the truck.

1. Description

The 2400 is a manually propelled pallet truck with a hydraulic lift, a 2,500 kg rated capacity, and forks 1,150 mm long. The tiller folds for storage and the pump releases through a trigger on the handle.

3. Operation

Approach the pallet squarely, with the forks level and lowered. Push the forks fully under the pallet before pumping. Pump the tiller until the load clears the floor by the amount needed, then move the truck at walking pace.

  1. Check that the pallet is sound and the load is stable on it.
  2. Lower the forks fully and align the truck with the pallet entry.
  3. Push the forks under the pallet until the tiller is close to the load.
  4. Pump the tiller to raise the load; do not exceed the rated capacity.
  5. Hold the tiller in the neutral position and move the truck slowly.
  6. Lower the load by pressing the release trigger; lower fully before storage.

4. Maintenance

Carry out the daily checks before each shift and the monthly checks as scheduled. Record every check in the maintenance log. A truck that fails a daily check must be withdrawn from service until it is repaired.
