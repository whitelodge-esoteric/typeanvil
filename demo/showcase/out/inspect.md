## TypeAnvil | Prince (reference) — per-page inspection (US Letter @ 300 DPI)

**This page is NOT scored; a visual difference from Prince is a hint, not a defect.**

Prince is rendered by `scripts/render-prince.sh` at the same geometry the showcase uses (US Letter, 0.75in margins) and rasterized at the same 300 DPI, so both sides are directly comparable by eye. Page counts can differ between the engines; that is recorded here, not scored.
