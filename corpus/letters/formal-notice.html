Take notice that, pursuant to clause 14 of the supply agreement between the parties dated 12 January 2025 (the "Agreement"), Harrowdale Supplies Ltd. hereby varies the Agreement with effect from 1 December 2026 in the manner set out below.

Variations

  1. 1. Schedule 2 (Delivery) is amended by replacing the monthly delivery window with a fortnightly window, so that the first window in each month begins on the first working day and the second on the fifteenth calendar day.
  2. 2. Schedule 4 (Prices) is amended by increasing the unit price of each listed line by 4.2 percent, reflecting the movement in the published input-cost index for the twelve months to 30 September 2026.
  3. 3. Clause 9 (Quality) is amended by adding a right of inspection at the supplier's premises on fourteen days' written notice, not more than twice in any calendar year.
  4. 4. Clause 12 (Term) is unchanged, and the Agreement continues until 11 January 2028 unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.

Effect and next steps

The variations take effect on 1 December 2026 unless the party served objects in writing within thirty days of service under clause 14.4. If no objection is received, the Agreement continues in full force as varied by this notice, and no further document is required to give the variations effect.

Any objection should be sent to the registered office shown above and marked for the attention of the company secretary. Any notice given under the Agreement shall be served in accordance with clause 18 and shall be deemed delivered in accordance with that clause.

This notice is served on all parties to the Agreement and a copy is retained by the company secretary.
