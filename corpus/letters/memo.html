Internal Memo

ToPlatform engineering, infrastructure, and support leads
FromPriya Raman, Director of Platform

SubjectPlatform migration, phase 2 — scope, schedule, and asks

This memo sets out the scope and schedule for phase 2 of the platform migration and the decisions I need from each of you by the end of next week. It supersedes the verbal plan circulated after the September offsite; where the two differ, this memo governs.

Where we are

Phase 1 closed on schedule. The rendering service now runs on the new runtime in production, and the error rate has held below the target for four weeks. The remaining work is the storage and retrieval layer and the cutover of the legacy scheduler.

Phase 2 scope

  1. Migrate the document store to the new object layout, with a dual-write period of two weeks and a verified back-fill.
  2. Replace the legacy scheduler with the queue-based dispatcher already validated in staging.
  3. Cut over the support tooling to the new admin API and retire read access to the old console.
  4. Complete the runbook updates and the on-call training.

Schedule

  • Week 41: document-store dual write begins; back-fill runs in the background.
  • Week 43: scheduler cutover; legacy path disabled.
  • Week 44: support tooling cutover and on-call training.
  • Week 45: decommission the legacy components and close the migration.

Asks

From platform engineering: confirm the dual-write compatibility matrix by Wednesday. From infrastructure: reserve the extra storage capacity for the back-fill window. From support: nominate two people for the on-call training in week 44.

If any dependency is at risk, raise it in the migration channel before Friday so we can re-plan while there is still slack in the schedule. I will hold a short sync every Monday until the migration closes.
