Data Sheet — Fernwood Cloud Platform

Enterprise tier · Rev. 4, October 2026 · For technical evaluation · All figures are service-level targets, not guarantees
At a glance99.95% availability target · 1 TB included storage · SAML and SCIM · regional data residency in three regions.

The Fernwood Cloud Platform is a managed document-infrastructure service with a documented API for rendering, storage, and retrieval. The enterprise tier adds single sign-on, audit logging, and a contractual data-residency commitment. This sheet states the technical characteristics a buyer needs to evaluate the service.
